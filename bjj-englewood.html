Getting here from Englewood

River Road north. Simple.

From most of Englewood: take River Road north or McCall Road east to US-41, then head north toward Punta Gorda. We're on US-41 with visible signage and free parking in front.

01

From Englewood via River Road

Head north on River Road (SR-776) toward Port Charlotte. At US-41, turn south toward Punta Gorda. We're on US-41 — approximately 20–22 minutes total from central Englewood.

02

From Englewood Beach / Manasota Key area

Take Beach Road to McCall Road, then north on US-41 or River Road toward Punta Gorda. The drive is typically 22–25 minutes. Lightest traffic on Saturday mornings.

03

Parking & arrival

Free parking in front of the building. Come 15 minutes early for your first class, tell whoever's at the front it's your first visit, and we'll take care of everything from there.
