What to expect

After you hit submit.

01

We reply, fast

You'll hear back from us within a few hours during business hours, or first thing the next morning if it's late. Probably a text — that's how most of our parents prefer it.

02

We pick a class together

Tell us which class works for your schedule, and we'll save you a spot. You don't have to know which program is right — that's our job.

03

You show up. We do the rest.

Loaner gi, mat space, water, friendly faces — all set. Show up 15 minutes early, sign a quick waiver, and we'll get you started.

04

Decide if it's for you

No pressure either way. We won't follow up with a sales pitch. If you want to come back, sign up online whenever you're ready. If not, we still hope to see you around town.
