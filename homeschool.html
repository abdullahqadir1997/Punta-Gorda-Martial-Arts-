What they actually learn

A real curriculum,
not just "playing on mats."

01

Foundational technique

Posture, base, hip movement, breakfalls. The same vocabulary world champions use — taught in language a 7-year-old understands.

02

Self-defense in real situations

What to do if someone grabs your wrist, your shirt, your bag. Not theoretical — practiced live, repeatedly, until it's reflex.

03

Belt progression you earn

Stripes and belts are awarded based on demonstrated skill, not time served. Kids feel the difference between participation and accomplishment.

04

Conditioning that's actually fun

Shrimping races, animal-walk warmups, takedown games. They'll come home tired in the good way.

05

How to lose well

Homeschool kids sometimes miss out on healthy competition. Our positional rounds teach them to compete, get tapped, reset, and try again — without it being a big deal.

06

Social skills with new peers

Working with kids of different ages, sizes, and personalities is a feature here, not a bug. They build friendships across the homeschool community.
