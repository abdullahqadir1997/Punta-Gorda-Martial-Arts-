Why people stay

Six things that keep
this class addictive.

/01

Real Self-Defense

BJJ is the only martial art proven to work for smaller people against larger attackers. You'll learn what actually works — not what looks cool in movies.

/02

Incredible Conditioning

Strength, cardio, mobility, and grit — all in one class. Most members lose 10–15 pounds in their first three months without even trying.

/03

Mental Toughness

Get comfortable being uncomfortable. The lessons you learn on the mat — patience, problem-solving, calm under pressure — bleed into everything else you do.

/04

Beginner-Friendly Format

Every class starts with fundamentals. We don't throw white belts to the wolves. You'll learn slowly, properly, and at your own pace.

/05

Friends, Not Just Members

You'll meet doctors, electricians, teachers, retirees, and college students — all on the same mat, all on a first-name basis. It's the friend group you didn't know you needed.

/06

Compete or Don't

Tournaments are optional. Always. We'll support you 100% if you want to compete — and respect you 100% if you don't.

A typical class

What your first hour on the mat looks like.

No surprises. No initiation rituals. Just a properly structured class designed to make you want to come back tomorrow.

View Class Schedule →
  • 1

    Warm-Up (10 min)

    Movement-based warm-up that doubles as fundamental drilling. By the time you're warm, you've already learned something.

  • 2

    Technique of the Day (25 min)

    One technique, broken down step-by-step, demonstrated multiple times, then drilled with a partner until it sticks.

  • 3

    Positional Sparring (15 min)

    Apply what you just learned in controlled, beginner-friendly rounds. We dial intensity to your level — always.

  • 4

    Open Rolls — optional (10 min)

    Want to test yourself? Stay for live rolling. Want to head home and stretch? Totally fine. Both are normal.
