For your 7–12 year old

The class that grows with them.

7-year-olds and 12-year-olds learn very differently. So we don't lump them in with the little kids or treat them like adults — we built a program just for this age.

Young Grapplers builds on the listening skills they've already developed and turns them loose on real Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. They'll learn techniques that work — for self-defense, for problem-solving, for the moment a kid at school decides to pick on someone smaller.

And here's what almost every parent tells us after the first month: "My kid is different now." More focused. More confident. More respectful. That's not us selling — that's them telling us.

What they'll learn

A real curriculum. Not just a glorified playdate.

Every class has a goal. Every belt has a progression. We treat your kid like they're capable — because they are.

  • ✓

    Real Self-Defense

    Escapes, takedowns, ground control — techniques that work for a smaller person against someone bigger. The same techniques police and military use.

  • ✓

    Anti-Bullying Skills

    Confidence is the #1 anti-bullying tool, and confidence is what jiu-jitsu builds. Bullies almost always pick the path of least resistance — and a confident kid stops being that path.

  • ✓

    Focus & Discipline

    Following instructions. Practicing patience. Doing hard things. Skills that show up everywhere — homework, sports, family life.

  • ✓

    Conflict De-Escalation

    The first lesson of jiu-jitsu is that you don't need to fight to win. We teach kids how to walk away — and how to handle it if walking away isn't an option.

  • ✓

    Respect & Etiquette

    "Yes sir." "Yes coach." "Thank you for the roll." Old-school respect, taught by example. It catches on quickly.

  • ✓

    Belt Progression

    Stripes and belts that mean something. Earned, not handed out. The pride your kid takes in their first stripe is something you'll never forget.
