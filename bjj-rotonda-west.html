For Rotonda West residents

Rotonda families already drive for the things that matter most.

Rotonda West is one of Southwest Florida's most community-minded neighborhoods — and the families here who train at PGMA say BJJ has become one of the best investments they make in their kids and themselves.

The drive from Rotonda West to Punta Gorda is about 20 minutes via Rotonda Blvd east to SR-776 and north on US-41. It's a relaxed, scenic route — and by the time you've been training for a month, you stop noticing it entirely. Several Rotonda families carpool with Englewood neighbors and make Saturday morning a regular community event.

We offer programs starting at age 5, six days a week, with experienced instructors who built this academy from the ground up with families like yours in mind. Rotonda West kids and adults are welcome on the mat today.

Why Rotonda West families choose us

Four reasons Rotonda families
keep coming back.

01

No BJJ Academy Closer to Rotonda

Rotonda West doesn't have a dedicated Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu program. Punta Gorda Martial Arts is the nearest full-service BJJ academy — and the 20-minute drive on low-traffic roads makes it genuinely accessible.

02

A Community That Fits Rotonda's Culture

Rotonda West is built around community — and so are we. Many of our Rotonda members quickly become close with Englewood and Port Charlotte families who train the same days and times. It becomes a social circle, not just a gym.

03

Expert Instruction, Not Just a Fitness Class

Our instructors teach real Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu with a lineage that matters. Whether your goal is competition, fitness, self-defense, or just showing up consistently — the instruction here is calibrated to your actual goals.

04

Saturday Mornings Work Perfectly

Light traffic from Rotonda on Saturday mornings. Adult class at 9am, kids' programs back to back. Many Rotonda families combine PGMA with a morning in Punta Gorda — it's become their weekly ritual.

Getting here from Rotonda West

Rotonda Blvd east to US-41.

From Rotonda West: take Rotonda Blvd or Boundary Blvd east to SR-776 (McCall Road), then north to US-41 toward Punta Gorda. We're on US-41 with visible signage and free parking out front.

From central Rotonda West

Take Rotonda Blvd or Boundary Blvd east to SR-776 (McCall Road). Head north on McCall Road to US-41, then north toward Punta Gorda. Approximately 20–22 minutes total.

02

Via Englewood Road

Head east on Englewood Road to US-41, then north toward Punta Gorda. This route takes approximately 20–25 minutes from most parts of Rotonda West.
