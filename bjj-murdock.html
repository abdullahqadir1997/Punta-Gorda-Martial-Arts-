Getting here from Murdock

Kings Highway to US-41. Done.

From the Murdock area: head south on US-41 (Tamiami Trail) toward Punta Gorda. It's a single road — no transfers, no confusion. We're on US-41 with a visible sign and free parking out front.

01

From Murdock / Kings Highway area

Take US-41 (Tamiami Trail) south from Kings Highway toward Punta Gorda. Cross into Punta Gorda — we're on US-41, approximately 10–12 minutes from the Kings Highway corridor.

02

From north Murdock / Cochran Blvd area

Take Cochran Blvd or Midway Blvd west to US-41, then south toward Punta Gorda. Approximately 12–14 minutes from the northern Murdock residential areas.

03
