How it works

First time?
Read this first.

01

Pick a class

For adults brand new to BJJ, our Fundamentals classes are the right fit (Mon/Wed at 6pm, or Sat at 9am). Kids should come to the age group that matches.

02

Tell us you're coming

Hit the contact page or call. We'll save you a spot, get a quick health waiver done, and have a clean loaner gi ready for you.

03

Show up 15 min early

Park out front. Walk in. Tell whoever's at the desk it's your first class. We'll handle everything from there.

04

Train, leave, decide later

Take the class. We won't follow up with a sales pitch. If you want to come back, you'll know — and you can sign up online whenever you're ready.

Pick a class, we'll save you a spot

Try a class
on us.

You don't need any experience, any gear, or any commitment. You don't even need to know which class you want — we'll help you pick. Just say hi.
