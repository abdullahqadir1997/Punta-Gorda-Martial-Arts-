Our Origin

Built by coaches who remembered what it felt like to start.

Most martial arts schools forget what it's like to walk in for the first time — nervous, out of shape, wondering if everyone is going to be way better than you.

We didn't. That's why our white belts roll with our purple belts. That's why your first class isn't a sales pitch — it's a real class, with real instruction, with real people who want you to come back. That culture isn't an accident. It's the entire reason this place exists.

We opened our doors in Punta Gorda with one simple goal: build the friendliest, most technically-sound, most family-welcoming Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu academy on Florida's Gulf Coast. Several hundred members later, we're not slowing down.

What we believe

Four principles that
shape every class.

01

Technique Over Toughness

We don't reward the biggest or the most aggressive. We reward the most precise. That's how a 130-pound mom can submit a 220-pound man — and yes, that happens here.

02

Everyone Belongs

You don't have to be in shape to start. You don't have to be young, athletic, or coordinated. You have to show up. We'll handle the rest.

03

Coaches, Not Drill Sergeants

Yelling doesn't teach jiu-jitsu. Curiosity, patience, and good explanations do. Our instructors lead by being the kind of training partner you'd want.

04

Skill Over Trophies

Competing is great. Self-defense is greater. We don't sell stripes or chase tournament rankings — we build people who actually know what they're doing.

Our Journey

From one mat to a real academy.

2018

Doors Open

Punta Gorda Martial Arts opens with two coaches, a single mat, and roughly twelve curious students. The mission is simple: teach Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu the way it's supposed to be taught.

2020

Kids Program Launches

Tiny Warriors and Young Grapplers launch — and within months we're full. Parents finally have a kids' program that takes both the kids and the curriculum seriously.

2022

Homeschool BJJ Added

By popular request, we add daytime homeschool classes for ages 6–14. Real curriculum, real instruction — just on a homeschool schedule.

2024

500+ Members & Counting

Punta Gorda Martial Arts becomes the largest BJJ academy on the Gulf Coast — and somehow still feels like a family. We intend to keep it that way.
