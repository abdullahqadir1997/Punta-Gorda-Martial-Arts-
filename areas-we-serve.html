Charlotte County's BJJ Academy

One academy. The whole region.

We're located in Punta Gorda — but our members come from communities all across Charlotte County and Southwest Florida.

Port Charlotte is 10 minutes away. Englewood is 20. Rotonda West is 25. Many of our members make that drive two, three, or four times a week — because the instruction quality, the family culture, and the depth of our program offerings make it worth it.

Why Charlotte County families choose PGMA

There's no shortcut to
great instruction.

01

The Gulf Coast's Largest BJJ Academy

500+ active members, 20+ weekly classes, and 5 distinct programs. Whether you need Mighty Grapplers for your 5-year-old or advanced no-gi training for yourself, we have it — and the class sizes and mat space to do it right.

02

Black-Belt-Led Instruction

Our head instructors have decades of combined experience with a lineage that traces directly to the roots of Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. That level of teaching is genuinely rare in a market this size.

03

Family Culture That Actually Means Something

Parents train next to their kids. Spouses train together. The community we've built over eight years doesn't happen by accident — and it's one of the main reasons people keep driving from Port Charlotte, Englewood, and beyond.

04

87 Five-Star Reviews and Counting

Families from across Charlotte County have left reviews saying the same things: welcoming culture, patient instructors, and kids who beg to come back. That reputation travels 10, 15, 20 minutes in any direction.
