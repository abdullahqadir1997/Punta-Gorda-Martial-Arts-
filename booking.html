After you book.

You'll get a confirmation

Email and text confirmation arrives within seconds. Save it — it has the exact time, address, and what to bring.

02

Show up 15 min early

Park out front. Walk in. Tell us it's your first class. We'll have a loaner gi, a waiver, and a friendly face waiting.

Take the class, leave when it ends. We won't follow up with a sales pitch. If you want to come back, you'll know.
