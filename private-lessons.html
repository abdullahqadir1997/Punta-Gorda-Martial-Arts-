When the group class isn't enough

A class of one.

Group classes are where you build mileage. Private lessons are where you fix the things group classes never will — your specific guard, your competition gameplan, the bad habit you can't shake on your own.

Every private is taught by our head instructor. We'll spend the first 10 minutes on what you actually want to work on, then drill it until it's wired in. You leave with one specific thing you do better than you did when you walked in.

  • 60-minute sessions, fully tailored
  • Video review available on request
  • Schedule by appointment — daytime or evening
  • Members and non-members welcome

Who privates are for

If any of these
sound like you, we should talk.

🥇

Competitors

You have a tournament on the calendar. We'll build a gameplan around your A-game and patch the holes that have been costing you matches.

🚀

Beginners who want a fast start

Walk into your first group class already knowing how to move. Two or three privates before you start saves you a year of confusion.

🔄

Returning students

Coming back after a layoff, an injury, or a baby? Privates rebuild your fundamentals at your pace before you throw yourself back into rolls.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parents (and kids)

Some kids learn faster one-on-one. We do private lessons for kids 6+ — especially helpful for shy kids before they join a group class.

🛡️

Self-defense focus

You're not interested in tournaments — you want practical skills. We'll build a 4–6 session arc around real-world self-defense scenarios.

🧗

Stuck-at-blue-belt syndrome

You've been training a few years and feel plateaued. Privates are how you find what you've been missing and break through.
