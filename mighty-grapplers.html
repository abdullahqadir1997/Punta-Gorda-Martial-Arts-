Class

Built for the youngest

A 30-minute class designed for attention spans shorter than the class itself.

5- and 6-year-olds don't learn the same way 10-year-olds do. So we don't pretend they can.

Mighty Grapplers classes are 30 minutes of focused, game-based fundamentals. We teach listening, body awareness, and basic positions through games, animal walks, partner drills, and lots of high-fives. There's no yelling. There's no sitting still for 20 minutes. There's no "drop and give me ten."

What there IS: kids learning to take turns, follow simple instructions, fall safely, and feel proud of themselves — all wrapped up so they don't even realize they're learning a martial art.

Class structure

Lots of fun. Real learning.

Every class is age-appropriate — but make no mistake, they're learning real techniques. Just delivered in a way little brains can handle.

  • ✓

    Listening Skills

    "Eyes on coach." "Sit on the line." "Find a partner." We teach the foundations of being a great student — skills that show up in school, at home, everywhere.

  • ✓

    Body Control & Coordination

    Animal walks, rolls, balance games. They'll learn to control their bodies in space — a skill most kids don't get from screens.

  • ✓

    Stranger Awareness

    Practical safety lessons baked in. What to do if someone unfamiliar tries to grab their wrist. Who to run to. How to make noise.

  • ✓

    Confidence Through Wins

    Lots of small wins, lots of belt stripes earned. They walk out of every class taller than they walked in.

  • ✓

    Following Rules & Taking Turns

    Mat etiquette is just etiquette. The kids who learn how to take turns and follow rules here are the kids who do better in kindergarten.

  • ✓

    The First Real Techniques

    Hip escapes. Side control. Mount. Yes — real BJJ. They're more capable than people think, and they'll surprise you.
