Getting here from Deep Creek

Peachland to US-41. Done.

The route from Deep Creek is as simple as it gets — take Peachland Blvd or Cochran Blvd south to US-41, then head south toward Punta Gorda. We're on US-41 with visible signage and free parking out front.

01

From the Deep Creek / Peachland area

Head south on Peachland Blvd to US-41. Turn south on US-41 toward Punta Gorda. We're on US-41 — approximately 12–14 minutes total from the Peachland/US-41 intersection.

02

From Deep Creek / Cochran Blvd area

Take Cochran Blvd south to US-41. Turn south and continue into Punta Gorda. The drive is typically 13–15 minutes depending on your starting point in Deep Creek.

03

Parking & arrival

Free parking directly in front of the building. Walk in and tell whoever's at the desk it's your first visit — we'll take care of everything from there. Come 15 minutes early for your first class.
